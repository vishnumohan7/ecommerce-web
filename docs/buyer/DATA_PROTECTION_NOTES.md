# Data protection notes

Merchants must confirm controller/processor roles, privacy notices, cookie categories and retention schedules with UK counsel. Consent changes are append-only. DSAR exports cover profile, addresses, orders, reviews, notification history and consent history.

Erasure removes addresses, wishlists, tokens, push subscriptions, preferences and reviews, and tombstones the user. Orders, invoices, payments, refunds and delivery/age-check evidence are retained for accounting, fraud, contractual and licensing-compliance obligations; direct identifiers and address snapshots are replaced with deterministic redaction markers. Retention periods must match the merchant’s documented lawful basis.
